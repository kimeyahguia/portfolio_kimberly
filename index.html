01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

DATA ↔ DESIGN ↔ PEOPLE

I'm a fourth-year BSIT student specializing in Business Analytics — someone who's just as comfortable in a spreadsheet as in a design file, and just as comfortable presenting to a room as coordinating a team.

I enjoy working in the space between data, design, and people — pulling insight out of information, shaping it into something usable, and helping a team actually get it done. I'm not the deepest developer in the room, but I can understand a problem, work with the data behind it, design the experience around it, and help lead a team toward a solution.

My interests span:

  • Business Intelligence
  • Data Visualization
